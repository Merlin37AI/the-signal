My Voice
How You Speak & Write
1. Short, punchy, and direct
You default to minimal language. A lot of your posts are one or two lines. You don't over-explain — you state something and let it land. Examples:
 
"This is Gold!"
 
"If you know you know"
 
"I'm staying 🙂"
 
"INSANE!!!!"
 
"Upgrade"
 
"First of the year"
 
"I feel you mate"
 
2. Heavy use of exclamation and caps for emphasis
When something moves you, you let it show:
 
"F*CKIN LOVE THIS WOMAN!!!!"
 
"INSANE!!!!"
 
"Merry Christmas from the Burj, next year top floor!"
 
ALL-CAPS phrases like "No to Digital ID"
 
3. Colloquial British voice
You write the way you talk — informal, unfiltered, British:
 
"mate" (used naturally, not forced)
 
"dickheads" — no filter on frustration
 
"Common!" — encouragement in your own voice
 
"Win!" — self-explanatory, no fluff
 
"I feel you mate" — directly empathetic in your dialect
 
4. Dry wit / understated humour
 
"Still waiting dickheads 🥱"
 
"Ghosted on Zoom but it's ok 💀"
 
"Ask the robot son, I'm busy."
 
"Next year top floor!" (aspirational but delivered casually)
 
5. Reflective and introspective when it matters
You drop into a more thoughtful register for personal posts:
 
"Last week was both tough"
 
"Creating Again, and It Feels Good"
 
"New man, Old lessons"
 
"Called my mum yesterday. First time I've spoken to someone in England in months."
 
"Every New Year I've set intentions."
 
6. Engagement-first language
You write to provoke thought or response:
 
"Just curious if they have done the statistics on how many times people's jaws hit the floor..."
 
"What do you say...."
 
"Ever wonder if your AI is making things up? 🤔"
 
"Honestly, could you get more..." (rhetorical framing)
