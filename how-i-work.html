How I work

Diagnosis before prescription.

No tool recommendations on day one. I find what's costing you time and money first. Only then do we talk about fixing it.

Most consultants sell you the answer before they've understood the question.

I don't. Eight years inside enterprise IT and operations taught me that the expensive problem is rarely the obvious one. So I lead with diagnosis. Three things hold true on every engagement.

01

Diagnose, don't pitch

I'm not a vendor. I have nothing to sell you but a clear read on where your operation leaks. If AI isn't the answer, I'll say so.

02

Proof, not promises

Every recommendation comes with a number attached. If we can't measure the gain, we don't call it a win.

03

Process before software

Most "AI problems" are process problems wearing a costume. I fix the workflow first. Tools come last, if at all.

The engagement

Three ways in. One path.

Clarity Conversation

A straight talk. Where's the drag, what's it costing, is there a case to dig deeper. No pitch, no obligation.

AI Clarity Session

A focused STOIC diagnostic on one part of the business. You leave with a written read on what's broken and what to fix first.

Build & Retainer

When the diagnosis says act, I scope, build and implement the fix, or stay on as ongoing advisory. Always measured.

What you get

  • A written diagnosis you own, in plain English
  • The operational leak quantified in £ and hours
  • A ranked list of fixes, process first and tech last
  • Data, risk and governance handled (UK GDPR + UAE)
  • One next action, never a 40-slide deck

What I won't do

  • Recommend a tool before I've found the problem
  • Sell you software you don't need
  • Bury the answer in jargon or hype
  • Promise savings I can't measure
  • Hand you a plan you can't act on Monday
Control what you can control. Measure the rest.
